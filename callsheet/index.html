CallsheetWhat it does, on one page

Walk in prepared. Walk out with the follow-up written.

Callsheet is a sales assistant that runs in Claude. It preps each call from public sources, drafts the follow-up, plans the drive and logs the expenses. Nothing is sent, booked or written to a CRM until the rep approves it.

Before the call

Prep

  • Cited account brief from SEC filings, job posts, news from the last 90 days and what peers filed, with likely effects marked "our analysis"
  • Contact card from the person's professional public record only
  • Morning digest at 7 am: every meeting, leave-by times, what needs you
  • Practice mode: Claude plays the buyer, then scores you
prep northwind
After the call

Follow up

  • Email, CRM note and task drafted from your notes or a transcript
  • One approval card with a fingerprint covers all of it
  • Send now or later; held automatically if they reply first
  • Catch-up and reply drafts: "where are we with Northwind?"
follow up on my northwind call
On the road

Plan

  • Best order for the day; set meetings keep their times
  • Group the week by area to cut miles, with reschedule notes to approve
  • Directions to meetings and company offices, never homes
  • Live gas prices near the next stop
plan my day
Any time

Expense

  • Log in plain words: "parking $18", a receipt photo, mileage
  • 26 categories, refunds, foreign currency and card statements
  • Flags, not refusals: a lunch without a purpose is logged and flagged
  • Send to Concur or Expensify after a preview, never twice
send my expenses to Concur

The rules it follows

  • Nothing goes out unseen. Emails, invites, CRM writes and expense submissions each need an explicit approval of the exact content.
  • Every claim has a source, or it moves to a "Verify before the call" list. Priorities from job posts need 2 or more postings and are labelled as hypotheses.
  • Professional record only. No home addresses, family, personal accounts or people-search sites.
  • Limited CRM writes. Notes, tasks and logged activity only. It never changes stage, amount or close date.
  • Instructions hidden in web pages are ignored and flagged to the rep.
